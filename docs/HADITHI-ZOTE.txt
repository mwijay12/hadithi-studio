HADITHI 1 / 6 — SAFARI YA SIMBA
==================================================================
Jina (SW)      : Safari ya Simba
Title (EN)     : Simba's Safari
Mwandishi      : Hadithi za Jadi
Aina           : Ngano
Umri           : Miaka 3-5
Kiwango        : Mtoto mdogo
Nyota          : 4.8   ·   Wasomaji: 1240

Muhtasari (SW) : Simba anaamka asubuhi moja na kuamua kwenda safari. Njiani anakutana na Twiga, Tembo na Sungura — na anajifunza kwamba safari nzuri kuliko zote ni ile unayosafiri na marafiki.

Synopsis (EN)  : Simba wakes up one morning and decides to go on a safari. Along the way he meets Giraffe, Elephant and Hare — and learns that the best safari of all is the one you take with friends.

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 1 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Simba aliamka asubuhi mapema. Jua lilikuwa linachomoza juu ya Serengeti. "Leo nitaenda safari!" alisema Simba kwa furaha.

  [EN] Simba woke up early one morning. The sun was rising over the Serengeti. "Today I am going on a safari!" said Simba happily.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Jua

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 2 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Njiani alikutana na Twiga. "Habari Twiga! Ungependa kuja nami?" Twiga aliinua shingo yake ndefu na kutabasamu. "Ndiyo, lakini mimi hukimbia pole pole," alisema.

  [EN] On the way he met Giraffe. "Hello Giraffe! Would you like to come with me?" Giraffe raised her long neck and smiled. "Yes, but I run quite slowly," she said.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Twiga

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 3 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Wakasafiri pamoja. Walikutana na Tembo. "Karibuni marafiki!" alisema Tembo kwa sauti kubwa. Tembo alitembea mbele yao, akifungua njia kwa mwili wake mkubwa.

  [EN] They travelled on together. They met Elephant. "Welcome, friends!" said Elephant in a big voice. Elephant walked in front of them, opening the path with his huge body.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Tembo

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 4 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Hapo walikutana na Sungura mdogo. "Msiende upande ule!" alisema Sungura. "Mto umejaa maji, na mvua inakuja." Simba alimsikiliza Sungura.

  [EN] Then they met little Hare. "Do not go that way!" said Hare. "The river is full, and rain is coming." Simba listened to Hare.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Sungura

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 5 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Mvua ikanyesha! Wote wakakimbia mpaka kwenye pango. Wakati mvua inanyesha, Twiga alisimulia hadithi, Tembo akaimba, na Sungura akachekesha kila mtu.

  [EN] The rain came down! They all ran to a cave. While the rain fell, Giraffe told stories, Elephant sang, and Hare made everyone laugh.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Pango

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 6 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Jioni walirudi nyumbani wote pamoja. "Safari nzuri kuliko zote," alisema Simba, "ni ile unayosafiri na marafiki." Kisha akalala chini ya mti, akiwa na furaha.

  [EN] In the evening they all went home together. "The best safari of all," said Simba, "is the one you take with friends." Then he lay down under a tree, happy.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Simba

HADITHI 2 / 6 — MLIMA KILIMANJARO
==================================================================
Jina (SW)      : Mlima Kilimanjaro
Title (EN)     : Mount Kilimanjaro
Mwandishi      : Juma Mkoba
Aina           : Historia ya tanzania
Umri           : Miaka 6-8
Kiwango        : Darasa 1-2
Nyota          : 4.7   ·   Wasomaji: 980

Muhtasari (SW) : Safari ya kupanda mlima mrefu zaidi Afrika. Neema na babu yake wanatembea kwa miguu kutoka msituni, hadi jangwani, hadi theluji ya kilele — na babu anasimulia hadithi ya mlima.

Synopsis (EN)  : A climb up the tallest mountain in Africa. Neema and her grandfather walk from the forest, through the desert, up to the snow at the summit — and Grandfather tells the story of the mountain.

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 1 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Kilimanjaro ni mlima mrefu zaidi barani Afrika. Uko Tanzania, karibu na mji wa Moshi. Mlima huu una kilele kinachoitwa Kibo, juu sana angani.

  [EN] Kilimanjaro is the tallest mountain in Africa. It stands in Tanzania, near the town of Moshi. The mountain has a peak called Kibo, very high in the sky.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Kibo

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 2 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Neema alienda na babu yake. "Babu, kwa nini kilele kina theluji wakati hapa kuna joto?" aliuliza. Babu alicheka. "Kwa sababu juu ni baridi kushinda chini," alisema.

  [EN] Neema went with her grandfather. "Babu, why does the peak have snow when it is hot down here?" she asked. Grandfather laughed. "Because up there it is colder than down here," he said.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Theluji

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 3 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Wakaanza kupanda. Kwanza msitu wenye mvua — miti mirefu na nyani wanaoruka. Kisha nyasi na maua ya rangi nyingi. Kisha jangwa la mawe.

  [EN] They began to climb. First a rainy forest — tall trees and monkeys leaping. Then grass and flowers of many colours. Then a desert of stones.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Msitu

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 4 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Usiku walipumzika kwenye kambi. Neema aliangalia juu. "Babu, nyota ni nyingi kuliko nilivyodhani!" Babu alimwambia kwamba watu wa kale walitumia nyota kupata njia.

  [EN] At night they rested at a camp. Neema looked up. "Babu, there are more stars than I thought!" Grandfather told her that people long ago used the stars to find their way.

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 5 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Baridi ilizidi, lakini walifika kileleni asubuhi. Theluji iling'aa kama chumvi. Neema aligusa na kupiga kelele: "Baridi!" Babu alicheka tena.

  [EN] The cold grew, but they reached the summit in the morning. The snow shone like salt. Neema touched it and shouted: "It is cold!" Grandfather laughed again.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Kilele

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 6 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Walishuka wakiwa na furaha. "Mlima ni mrefu," alisema Neema, "lakini hatua moja kwa wakati mmoja unafika." Babu alimkumbatia.

  [EN] They came down happy. "The mountain is tall," said Neema, "but one step at a time, you arrive." Grandfather hugged her.

HADITHI 3 / 6 — NYOTA ZA USIKU
==================================================================
Jina (SW)      : Nyota za Usiku
Title (EN)     : Stars of the Night
Mwandishi      : Hadithi za Jadi
Aina           : Maadili
Umri           : Miaka 6-8
Kiwango        : Darasa 1-2
Nyota          : 4.6   ·   Wasomaji: 720

Muhtasari (SW) : Baraka anaishi kijijini ambako hakuna umeme. Usiku mmoja bibi yake anamwonyesha kwamba anga ni ramani ya zamani kuliko vitabu vyote — na kila swali huzaa jibu jingine.

Synopsis (EN)  : Baraka lives in a village with no electricity. One night his grandmother shows him that the sky is a map older than any book — and that every question gives birth to another.

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 1 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Kijiji chetu hakina umeme. Usiku, tunaketi nje kwenye mkeka na kuangalia juu. Bibi alisema, "Anga ndiyo runinga ya watu wa kale."

  [EN] Our village has no electricity. At night we sit outside on a mat and look up. Grandmother said, "The sky is the television of the old people."

  Vitu vya kugusa: Nyota

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 2 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Nilimuuliza bibi, "Nyota ni ngapi?" Bibi alicheka. "Zaidi ya mchanga wa bahari. Watu wa kale walizipanga kama picha angani."

  [EN] I asked grandmother, "How many stars are there?" Grandmother laughed. "More than the sand of the sea. Long ago people arranged them like pictures in the sky."

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 3 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Bibi alinyoosha kidole. "Unaona nyota nne zilizoungana kama msalaba? Hiyo ni Msalaba wa Kusini. Kama ukiifuata, unapata upande wa kusini."

  [EN] Grandmother pointed. "Do you see four stars joined like a cross? That is the Southern Cross. If you follow it, you find the south."

  Vitu vya kugusa: Msalaba wa Kusini

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 4 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Nikamuuliza, "Kwa nini mwezi unabadilika umbo?" Bibi alisema, "Mwezi unazunguka dunia. Jua linawaka upande mmoja tu, kwa hivyo tunaona sehemu tofauti kila usiku."

  [EN] I asked her, "Why does the moon change shape?" Grandmother said, "The moon goes around the earth. The sun lights only one side, so we see a different part each night."

  Vitu vya kugusa: Mwezi

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 5 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Usiku ule nilijifunza kitu kikubwa: kila kitu ninachojua kilianza na swali moja. Nikaamua kuwa mtaalam wa nyota.

  [EN] That night I learned something big: everything I know began with one question. I decided to become a star scientist.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Bibi

HADITHI 4 / 6 — NGOMA YA FURAHA
==================================================================
Jina (SW)      : Ngoma ya Furaha
Title (EN)     : The Happy Drum
Mwandishi      : Fatuma Ally
Aina           : Sanaa
Umri           : Miaka 6-8
Kiwango        : Darasa 1-2
Nyota          : 4.5   ·   Wasomaji: 640

Muhtasari (SW) : Ngoma kubwa ilikaa kimya kwa miaka mingi. Siku moja Juma aliipiga mara moja — na sauti ile ikawakusanya watu wote wa kijiji walioachana kwa muda mrefu.

Synopsis (EN)  : A big drum had stayed silent for many years. One day Juma struck it once — and that sound gathered all the villagers who had grown apart.

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 1 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Kwenye ukumbi wa kijiji kulikuwa na ngoma kubwa. Ilikuwa imetulia kimya kwa miaka mingi. Kila mtu alikuwa na shughuli zake, na hakuna aliyekumbuka kuipiga.

  [EN] In the village hall there was a big drum. It had rested silent for many years. Everyone had their own work, and nobody remembered to strike it.

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 2 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Juma alipapasa vumbi kwa kitambaa. Akapiga mara moja: "Pa!" Sauti ikatoka ukumbini, ikavuka uwanja, ikafika shambani.

  [EN] Juma wiped the dust away with a cloth. He struck it once: "Pa!" The sound left the hall, crossed the playground and reached the fields.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Ngoma

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 3 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Mzee Hamisi alifika mbio. "Hii ni ngoma ya babu yangu!" alisema. Akamfundisha Juma mdundo: "Kamoja, kamoja, pawili." Juma alijaribu, akakosea, akajaribu tena.

  [EN] Mzee Hamisi arrived at a run. "This is my grandfather's drum!" he said. He taught Juma the rhythm: "Once, once, twice." Juma tried, made a mistake, and tried again.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Mdundo

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 4 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Watu walifika wengi. Mama walichemsha chai, watoto walicheza, bibi alipiga nyimbo za zamani. Uani palikuwa na furaha ambayo haikuwepo kwa muda mrefu.

  [EN] Many people came. Mothers made tea, children played, grandmothers sang the old songs. There was a joy in the yard that had been missing for a long time.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Wanacheza

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 5 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Juma alijifunza kwamba ngoma moja hupiga mioyo mingi. Kuanzia siku ile, kila Jumamosi, kijiji chetu hupiga ngoma pamoja.

  [EN] Juma learned that one drum plays many hearts. From that day on, every Saturday, our village drums together.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Juma

HADITHI 5 / 6 — KUKU NA MWEWE
==================================================================
Jina (SW)      : Kuku na Mwewe
Title (EN)     : The Hen and the Hawk
Mwandishi      : Hadithi za Jadi
Aina           : Ngano
Umri           : Miaka 3-5
Kiwango        : Mtoto mdogo
Nyota          : 4.4   ·   Wasomaji: 1560

Muhtasari (SW) : Zamani Kuku na Mwewe walikuwa marafiki na majirani. Kuku aliazima sindano ya Mwewe, akaipoteza — na hapo ndipo ufugaji wetu wa kuku ulipoanza kuchimba udongo.

Synopsis (EN)  : Long ago Hen and Hawk were friends and neighbours. Hen borrowed Hawk's needle and lost it — and that is how our hens began scratching the ground.

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 1 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Zamani sana, Kuku na Mwewe walikuwa majirani. Wakakaa pamoja, wakala pamoja, wakacheka pamoja.

  [EN] Long, long ago, Hen and Hawk were neighbours. They sat together, ate together and laughed together.

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 2 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Siku moja Kuku alitaka kushona mfuko wa nafaka. Akamwomba Mwewe, "Niazime sindano yako." Mwewe alimpa kwa furaha.

  [EN] One day Hen wanted to sew a grain bag. She asked Hawk, "Lend me your needle." Hawk gave it to her happily.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Mwewe

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 3 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Kuku alishona, akashona, akashona. Ghafla sindano ikaanguka kwenye nyasi. Akaichimba, akaichimba — haikuonekana. Ndiyo maana kuku huchimba udongo hata leo.

  [EN] Hen sewed and sewed and sewed. Suddenly the needle fell into the grass. She scratched and scratched — it was not there. That is why hens scratch the ground even today.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Kuku

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 4 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Mwewe alikuja kwa sindano yake. Kuku hakuwa na kitu cha kumpa. "Nilikuamini!" Mwewe alilia kwa hasira. "Kuanzia leo nitakufuata milele."

  [EN] Hawk came for her needle. Hen had nothing to give. "I trusted you!" Hawk cried angrily. "From today I will chase you forever."

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 5 / 5
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Kuanzia siku ile, kila Mwewe aonapo kuku anawafukuza. Tunapowaona angani, tunakumbuka: kile ulichoazima, kirudishe.

  [EN] From that day, whenever Hawk sees hens he chases them. When we see him in the sky, we remember: return what you borrow.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Somo

HADITHI 6 / 6 — CHAPATI ZA MAMA
==================================================================
Jina (SW)      : Chapati za Mama
Title (EN)     : Mama's Chapati
Mwandishi      : Neema Kessy
Aina           : Maadili
Umri           : Miaka 9-12
Kiwango        : Darasa 3-4
Nyota          : 4.6   ·   Wasomaji: 830

Muhtasari (SW) : Zawadi ana miaka kumi na anataka kujifunza kupika chapati. Siku moja anagundua kwamba chapati ya kwanza ni ya kujifunza, na ile ya pili ni ya kushiriki.

Synopsis (EN)  : Zawadi is ten and wants to learn to cook chapati. One day she discovers that the first chapati is for learning, and the second is for sharing.

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 1 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Kila Ijumaa jioni, mama yangu hupika chapati. Harufu yake huenea kwenye mtaa mzima wa Arusha. Siku moja nikamwambia, "Mama, nifundishe."

  [EN] Every Friday evening my mother cooks chapati. Its smell spreads through the whole street in Arusha. One day I told her, "Mama, teach me."

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 2 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Mama alipima unga kwa kikombe: vikombe vitatu vya unga, kikombe kimoja cha maji ya moto, chumvi kidogo. Nilihesabu kwa sauti. Hesabu ya jikoni ni hesabu halisi.

  [EN] Mama measured the flour with a cup: three cups of flour, one cup of hot water, a little salt. I counted out loud. Kitchen counting is real counting.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Kipimo

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 3 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Mama alifunika unga kwa kitambaa. "Tusubiri dakika ishirini," alisema. Nilisubiri, nikasubiri. "Unga unahitaji kupumzika," aliniambia, "kama sisi tu."

  [EN] Mama covered the dough with a cloth. "Let us wait twenty minutes," she said. I waited and waited. "Dough needs to rest," she told me, "just like us."

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 4 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Nikaanza kusukuma kwa mti wa kusukuma. Chapati ya kwanza ikaungua, ikawa nyeusi. Nikaona aibu. Mama alicheka: "Hata mimi nilichoma cha kwanza."

  [EN] I began to roll with the rolling pin. My first chapati burned and turned black. I felt ashamed. Mama laughed: "Even I burned my first one."

  Vitu vya kugusa: Mama

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 5 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Chapati ya tano ikawa nzuri — duara, laini, yenye harufu nzuri. Mama akachukua nne, akaiweka kwenye sinia, na tukampelekea Bwana Salum mgonjwa.

  [EN] The fifth chapati turned out well — round, soft and sweet-smelling. Mama took four, put them on a tray, and we carried them to the sick Bwana Salum.

  --------------------------------------------------------------
  UKURASA 6 / 6
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  [SW] Bwana Salum alifungua mlango, akaona sinia, akatabasamu hata macho yakamngaa. Nikaandika kwenye daftari langu: chapati ya pili ni ya kushiriki.

  [EN] Bwana Salum opened the door, saw the tray, and smiled until his eyes shone. I wrote in my notebook: the second chapati is for sharing.

  Vitu vya kugusa: Uamuzi
